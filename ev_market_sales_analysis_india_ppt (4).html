What is an Electric Vehicle? 02 / 20

  • Pure Electric Drive: Powered entirely by electrochemical energy.
  • Battery & Motor: Replaces fuel engine with powertrain pack.
  • Zero Tailpipe Emissions: Clean urban air quality with high torque.

Powertrain Architecture

Battery Inverter Motor Wheels
Speaker Note: "An electric vehicle eliminates the internal combustion engine, channeling electrical energy from high-voltage battery cells through an inverter to drive electric motors directly."

Price Elasticity vs Sales Volume 15 / 20

Inverse Price Elasticity

"Mass adoption strictly peaks at affordable price brackets."

• Sub-₹1.5 Lakh 2Ws generate 82% of two-wheeler purchases.
• ₹10L–₹16L forms the optimal sweet spot for 4W personal cars.

Speaker Note: "Our Power BI scatter plot analysis confirms high price elasticity; models crossing the ₹20 Lakh barrier experience lower unit sales volume."

Battery Capacity vs Driving Range 16 / 20

Statistical Correlation

"High positive linear correlation (r = +0.92)"

Average efficiency yields ~7.8 km per 1 kWh of battery pack in passenger 4Ws.

Pack Size Benchmarks

• 2-Wheelers: 3.0 – 4.0 kWh (~120 km)
• 4-Wheelers: 30 – 50 kWh (~320+ km)

Speaker Note: "Statistical correlation yields an r-value of 0.92, verifying that battery pack capacity in kilowatt-hours is the single largest physical determinant of driving range."
